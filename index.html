How it works

From live data to an executed plan, without a planner in the loop.

1

Encode the operation

Jobs, lines, SKUs, vehicles, stops, shifts and demand, kept current by API or CSV.

2

Set the trade-offs

What a late order, a changeover minute, a stock-out or an hour of overtime costs you.

3

Compute and compare

The engine returns the best plan it finds, always next to what today's practice would cost.

4

Let it run itself

Plans that pass your guardrails are approved and executed. The rest are held and raised as alerts.

Five decisions, one autopilot

Each engine answers one operational question, and they chain together.

Manufacturing

  • Production sequencing across lines.
  • Family changeovers, maintenance windows and due dates.
  • Time-of-use energy tariffs in the cost.
  • Gantt view of the optimised plan and today's practice.

Supply chain and demand

  • Order quantity, safety stock and reorder point per SKU.
  • Working-capital budget as a hard limit.
  • Weekly demand forecast per SKU, checked against a naive forecast.

Logistics and retail

  • Vehicle routing with capacity, delivery windows and customer tiers.
  • Route map and per-vehicle load, distance and cost.
  • Store rosters that follow hourly demand.

Autopilot

  • Manual, supervised or autonomous, per decision.
  • Re-computes on a schedule, on every data change and on every disruption.
  • Guardrails: saving, late jobs, unserved stops, on-time, fill rate, coverage.
  • One switch pauses all automation.

Exceptions only

  • Disruptions: line breakdown, rush order, vehicle failure, same-day order, demand spike.
  • Alerts for held plans and failed executions, with a webhook for team chat.
  • Data-health checks that block unattended execution on bad data.
  • Audit trail of every step.

Around it

  • Decision chain: forecast, stock, production, routes and rosters in one run.
  • API for systems to push data and events and read the plan in force.
  • Reports with CSV export and a print layout.
  • Public site with a live demo and self-booked discovery sessions.
  • Runs on your own server; no online AI service is used.

Good to know: the scheduling, routing and rostering engines are heuristic searches that return the best plan found, not a mathematical proof of optimality. The ERP, MES, WMS, fleet and workforce connectors in this version are demo adapters that write to an outbox; a webhook connector sends decisions out for real, and integrations with specific systems are built per client. The platform does not send email.
